
>Def (ordering of $E_k$):

>Def (coordinate represntation):
>	Let $\prec$ be an ordering of $E_k$, and set $m:=C(k,2)$. Denote by $\omega_{\prec}:[m]\to E_k$ the unique bijection satifiying $i<j\Leftrightarrow \omega_\prec(i)\prec \omega_\prec(j)$ . A coordinate represntation of tournaments with respect to $\prec$ is the mapping $R_\prec:T_k\to\mathbb{Z}_2^{m}$ defined by $R_\prec(Y):=(Y(\omega_\prec(1)),...,Y(\omega_\prec(m))$.
>	
>	Note: existence and uniqueness of $\omega_\prec$ is seen by induction, since $E_k$ is finite :) YAY
