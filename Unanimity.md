
>Def (Unanimous function):
>	Let $f:S_k^n \to S_k$. We say that $f$ is unanimous, or that $f$ respects unanimous voting when for all $\sigma = \begin{pmatrix} \sigma_1 \\ \sigma_2 \\ \vdots \\ \sigma_n \end{pmatrix} \in S_k^n$, if $\sigma_i^{-1}(1) = \sigma_j^{-1}(1) \forall i,j \in [n]$ then $f\left(\sigma\right)^{-1}(1) = \sigma_1^{-1}(1)$. That is to say that if all the voters agree on who is the best candidate, then $f(\sigma)$ agrees and sends that candidate to $1$.


Other variations:
	$f$ respects any agreement
	$f$ respects $k$ agreements
	$f$ respects full agreement
