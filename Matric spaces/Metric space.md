>Def (A matric):
>	Let $X$ be a set. And $d:X^2\to\mathbb{R}$.
>	We say that $d$ is a matric on $X$ when:

>	>(1): $d$ is symetric. Meaning that $\forall x,y \in X: d(x,y) = d(y,x)$.

>	>(2): $d$ is absolutely positive. Meaning that $\forall x,y \in X: d(x,y) \geq 0 \vee d(x,y) = 0 \iff x = y$.

^6067ad

>	>(3): Triangle inequality ($\triangle ie$). Meaning that $\forall x,y,z \in X: d(x,z) \leq d(x,y) + d(y,z)$.

>Def (Matric space):
>	Let $X$ be a set. And $d:X^2\to\mathbb{R}$.
>	We say that $(X,d)$ is a matric space when $d$ [[#^344d53|is a matric]] on $X$.
