

>Def (Contracting projection / compatible pair):
>	Let $Y,Z$ be sets with [[Accumulation Scheme#^dc3a91|discrepencies]] $d_Y,d_Z$, let $P:Y\to Z$ and let $C\in[0,\infty)$. We say that $P$ is $C$-contracting from $d_Y$ to $d_Z$, or that the pair $(d_Y,d_Z)$ is $C$-compatible along $P$, when $$\forall y,y'\in Y:\quad d_Z\big( P(y),P(y') \big)\ \le\ C\cdot d_Y(y,y')$$
>	yaani: the projection is not allowed to *create* discrepency out of nothing. whatever disagreement survives downstairs was already present upstairs, up to the factor $C$.

>Def (Modulus of a projection):
>	[[#^cc0b17|In the same context]], let $\varphi:[0,\infty]\to[0,\infty]$ be non decreasing with $\varphi(0)=0$. We say that $P$ is $\varphi$ - controlled from $d_Y$ to $d_Z$ when $$\forall y,y'\in Y:\quad d_Z\big( P(y),P(y') \big)\ \le\ \varphi\big( d_Y(y,y') \big)$$
